Dust is a Morse code (CW) trainer for amateur radio operators, from your first two letters to contest speed. It sends groups, callsigns, words or a whole contest; you copy them by ear and see exactly what you got right, what you missed, and how fast you recognised each character.

Made by a ham, SO5KM. Free and open source: no ads, no account, no in-app purchases.

LEARN MORSE CODE THE KOCH WAY
• Koch method: start with two characters and unlock the next as you copy them
• Letter orders of LCWO and CW Academy, alphabetical, or your own
• Auto level moves you up, or back, by how your sessions go
• Farnsworth spacing and adjustable word spacing
• Presets for Novice, Intermediate, Advanced and Expert
• A guided setup walks you through your first session
• Listen mode: hear characters and groups without being scored

COPY WHAT YOU WILL HEAR ON THE AIR
• Letters, digits, mixed, callsigns or words
• Callsigns in six tiers, from real prefixes weighted the way you hear them
• Words from a starter list with the prosigns AR and BT, or your own list
• Adaptive speed and group size: faster and longer while you copy, slower and shorter when you miss
• Characters you miss come round more often
• Lock typing while sending to train head copy
• Speed and pitch ranges, repeats, and the shape of every dit and dah

A REAL BAND, NOT A CLEAN BEEP
• Band noise heard through a CW filter you can narrow and reshape
• QSB fading, QRN static and lightning crashes, signal-to-noise in dB
• AGC and RF gain that behave like a rig's
• Stations that key like people, not like a perfect keyer

CW CONTEST AND PILE-UP TRAINING
• Call CQ, copy the call and exchange, send TU, log the QSO
• CQ WPX, CQ WW, ARRL DX, ARRL Field Day, NA Sprint and a simple QSO
• One caller at a time, or a pile-up with QRM
• Ask with ? when you miss something; type K5? to bring back only the stations that fit
• Callers correct a wrong call, as real stations do
• New to contesting? Hear a whole QSO first
• Raw and verified scores, QSO rate and a log that marks every mistake
• Send from the keyboard, the on-screen keypad or paddles

PADDLES AND KEYER
• Iambic A, iambic B, Ultimatic or straight
• Key with a USB paddle adapter that types [ and ], or with the keyboard
• A sidetone shaped like a real transmitter's

SEE EXACTLY WHERE YOU STAND
• Accuracy and mastery for every letter
• Recognition time per character, with instant character recognition (ICR) bands
• Mistakes: which characters you confuse, down to the dit or dah
• Character pairs that trip you up
• Copy speed in WPM and copy span, learned from every session
• Endurance: when your copy starts to fade in a long session
• Calendar, streaks, history, a scoreboard with medals and personal records
• Back up and restore your practice

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Your practice stays on your device. Dust has no account and no tracking, and never sends your callsign or history anywhere.

Also runs in the browser and on Linux, Windows and macOS. Source code (GPLv3): github.com/mashu/dust

73 and good luck on the air!
